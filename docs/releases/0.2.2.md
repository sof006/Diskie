Shows how far a delete has got, and lets you stop it.

**Already running Diskie?** Click the version at the bottom right of the window to update now.

## New

- **Delete progress.** While deleting permanently, a bar above the list shows how many items are gone, how much space is freed, and the percentage done. **Stop** halts it before the next item; what is already deleted stays deleted, and Diskie tells you how much went.
- Moving to the Recycle Bin (Trash on Linux) shows a moving bar until it is done.

## Fixed

- Starting a second delete while one is running now asks you to wait, instead of running both at once.
- A delete that finishes after a rescan no longer removes the wrong item from the new results.
- Read-only folders are deleted too, not only read-only files.
