Fixes the right-click menu over the treemap.

**Already running Diskie?** Click the version at the bottom right of the window to update now.

## Fixed

- **Right-click menu items did nothing over the treemap.** When the menu opened on top of the treemap, *Move to Recycle Bin*, *Delete permanently*, *Show in Explorer* and *Copy path* closed the menu without doing anything. They now work wherever the menu opens.
- **Window icon on Windows.** Diskie's window now carries its own icon in the large and small sizes, for Task Manager, Alt+Tab and the taskbar.
