Diskie now runs on Linux.

**Already running Diskie on Windows?** It updates itself as usual; click the version at the bottom right to check now.

## Linux

- **Two downloads**, for x86-64 desktops with glibc 2.35 or newer (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 and later):
  - `Diskie-0.2.0-x86_64.AppImage`: make it executable and run it.
  - `Diskie-0.2.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`: unpack it and run `./install.sh`. That puts `diskie` in `~/.local/bin` and adds it to your applications menu.
- **Both keep themselves up to date**, checked against the same signing key as the Windows build.
- **Runs as your normal user.** Folders you can't read are marked "no access".
- **A scan stays on one file system.** Other mounts inside it, such as `/proc` or a USB stick, are tagged "other drive" and not entered. Double-click one to scan it on its own.
- **Files with several hard links are counted once.**
- **The sidebar lists your mounted drives** with their free space, including network shares and removable drives. In WSL it also lists the Windows drives.
- **Where the desktop doesn't draw a title bar** (GNOME, WSLg), Diskie draws its own:
  - drag the toolbar to move the window, and double-click it to maximize;
  - minimize, maximize and close buttons;
  - resizable edges.
- **Deleting moves items to the Trash.** Network mounts have no Trash, so Diskie won't delete there.

## Fixed

- **An update installed automatically could lose its safety net.** When the download finished more than 15 seconds after Diskie started, Diskie deleted the copy of the previous version that it keeps for a rollback. It now keeps that copy until the new version has been running for 15 seconds.
